01

Taking Sheldon — the checkpoints

Georgia does not require a rabies antibody titre test for dogs from at-risk countries like India, and imposes no quarantine when the rules are met — that's what makes it feasible for late November when Italy isn't. The only real timing gate is a valid rabies shot.

Step 1 · India

ISO microchip

15-digit ISO 11784/11785 chip, implanted before the rabies vaccination. Same number on every document.

Step 2 · India

Rabies vaccination — valid before travel

Georgia requires the primary rabies vaccine at least 30 days before arrival. For a late-Nov trip, have his rabies shot completed by about 23 October 2026.

the one date that governs everything
Step 3 · India

Parasite treatment

Deworming + tick/flea treatment before travel (sensible after the tick-fever); note it on his record.

Step 4 · India

AQCS Bangalore export certificate + NOC

Book AQCS ~1 week ahead; present Sheldon ~7 days before departure. Export certificate valid only 10 days. Keep the originals — they let you re-import him without quarantine.

Step 5 · Tbilisi

Arrival in Georgia

Entry via Tbilisi airport; National Food Agency may check documents + microchip. No titre, no quarantine when compliant.

Step 6 · Return

Bringing Sheldon home to India

Apply for an Advance NOC from AQCS ≥7 working days before flying back, under the Re-import category (Customs Circular 25/2013), using your original export docs — avoids the 15-day quarantine on fresh imports. Carry a Georgian vet health certificate dated close to departure.

What to cover — quaint, not commercial

Base · 3–4 nights

Old Tbilisi

Carved wooden balconies under the Narikala fortress; the domed sulphur bathhouses of Abanotubani; natural-wine bars; the Dry Bridge flea market; the Fabrika courtyard. Walkable, dog-tolerant, cheap.

Day trip · 40 min

Mtskheta

Ancient spiritual capital, UNESCO-listed: hilltop Jvari Monastery above two rivers, and 11th-century Svetitskhoveli cathedral. Quiet, sacred, an easy half-day.

2 nights · the drama

Georgian Military Road → Kazbegi

Ananuri fortress on its reservoir, the Gudauri viewpoint, then Stepantsminda where the Gergeti Trinity Church stands alone against 5,047 m Mt Kazbek. In snow, the defining image of Georgia.

2 nights · wine + a fairytale town

Kakheti & Sighnaghi

Walled hilltop "city of love," terracotta roofs, Caucasus views. Family qvevri wineries pour amber wine from buried clay; Bodbe convent among cypresses. Slow food, warm hosts, no crowds.

A calm 10–12 day shape: Tbilisi (3–4) → Mtskheta day trip → Kazbegi (2) → Kakheti/Sighnaghi (2) → Borjomi or Uplistsikhe day → back to Tbilisi (1–2). All low-altitude, driveable, Sheldon-friendly.

09

Driving & car rental

Georgia is genuinely easy and rewarding to self-drive — right-hand traffic, signs in Georgian and English on main routes, airport pickups, and Google Maps works fine. The catch is assertive local driving and snowy mountain roads to Kazbegi/Gudauri in late November — so you want winter tyres and nerve, or a driver for that leg.

The honest self-drive verdict

  • City & wine country (Kakheti, Mtskheta): easy, flat, well-signed — great self-drive with Sheldon in his carrier or a harness.
  • Georgian Military Road to Kazbegi: spectacular but a real mountain road; in snow it demands winter tyres and confidence. Many couples take a private driver for this one day.
  • Paperwork: carry an International Driving Permit (IDP) with your licence. Book online 2–3 days ahead — walk-up airport rates run 30–50% higher.
  • Insurance: third-party is included; add CDW/full cover (~₾30–60/day) for mountains. Deposit ~$180–360 blocked on a credit card.

01

Taking Sheldon — the checkpoints

Kazakhstan requires no rabies titre test for dogs of any origin, no import permit for one or two pets travelling with you, and no quarantine when compliant. The most relaxed of your feasible options. The only gate is a valid rabies shot.

Step 1 · India

ISO microchip

Step 2 · India

Rabies vaccination — valid before travel

the one date that governs everything
Step 3 · India

Vet health certificate (within 5 days)

International/EAEU-form certificate, government-vet signed — healthy + rabies-vaccinated, dated within 5 days of travel. In India this is folded into the AQCS export exam.

Book AQCS ~1 week ahead; present Sheldon ~7 days before departure. Export certificate valid 10 days. Keep the originals for re-import.

Step 5 · Almaty

Arrival in Kazakhstan

Enter via Almaty international airport; a vet officer may check chip + papers. No titre, no quarantine, no import permit for 1–2 pets.

Step 6 · Return

Bringing Sheldon home to India

What to cover — nature first, city as base

Base · the whole trip

Almaty, father of apples

The wild apple was born in these mountains ("Alma-Ata"). Leafy and food-obsessed: the Green Bazaar for horse-sausage, dried fruit & honey; the wooden Zenkov Cathedral (built without a single nail); Kok-Tobe hill by cable car.

½ day · up the mountain

Medeu & Shymbulak

Ride up past the Medeu skating rink to Shymbulak ski resort by gondola, into the Trans-Ili Alatau. Snow, spruce and a wall of peaks — dramatic scenery with zero trekking effort.

Day trip · the icon

Big Almaty Lake

A milky-turquoise glacial lake at ~2,500 m, cupped by snow peaks a short drive from town — ibex and golden eagles overhead. Frozen and stunning in late November (go with a 4WD/driver; the upper checkpoint near the Kyrgyz border needs a passport).

Long day / overnight · drama

Charyn Canyon + Kaindy & Kolsai

East of the city: the red-rock "Valley of Castles" at Charyn, then the Tian Shan lakes — Kaindy, with its eerie sunken spruce forest, and the Kolsai lakes in deep spruce valleys. Best as a guided 1–2 day trip in November.

A comfortable 10–12 day shape: Almaty base with day trips — Shymbulak/Medeu, Big Almaty Lake, a Charyn + Kaindy/Kolsai overnight, Turgen gorge, and slow city days for the bazaar, cathedral & cafés. Big nature, warm bed every night — kind to a young dog.

Driving & car rental

Be honest with yourselves here: Almaty in late November is not an easy self-drive for first-timers. The city is navigable, but winter brings snow and challenging roads, signage/police lean Russian/Kazakh, and the best nature sits on rough mountain and steppe roads that often need a 4WD and local know-how. A driver or guided day trips is the smart, low-stress default — often barely pricier than renting once you add winter risk.

The honest verdict

  • Around the city: self-drive is fine, but the Yandex Go app (cheap, reliable taxis) makes a rental unnecessary for city days.
  • Big Almaty Lake & the eastern lakes: hire a driver with a 4WD — the winter road to Kaindy from Saty needs it, and a guide handles the border checkpoint above BAL.
  • Paperwork if you rent: physical licence + IDP + a second photo ID + credit card for the deposit; book ahead as the fleet is small.
  • Winter caveat: snow, limited English on the road, and cash-only toll stretches — patience and a local driver save the holiday.
